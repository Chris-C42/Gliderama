# Credits

Gliderama is a love letter to John Calhoun's *Glider 4.0* and *Glider PRO*.

## Classic Houses

The houses in the *Classic Houses* campaign are adapted from the houses that ship with **Glider PRO**
(Casady & Greene, 1994), whose source code, graphics, sounds and houses John Calhoun released under the
GNU General Public License, version 2: <https://github.com/softdorothy/GliderPRO>. They are converted to
Gliderama's rooms, furniture and paper-plane physics, so they play differently from the originals.

House credits, from the Glider PRO release:

- Demo House and CD Demo House: John Calhoun and Kim Money
- Davis Station, Metropolis and Titanic: Jonathan Chin (alias Paul Finn) and John Calhoun
- Grand Prix, Leviathan, ImagineHouse PRO II and In The Mirror: Jonathan Chin (alias Paul Finn)
- Land of Illusion, Nemo's Market, Rainbow's End and SpacePods: Ward Hartenstein
- Slumberland: John Calhoun (first house and top of fourth house), Jonathan Chin (second house),
  Steve Sullivan (third house) and Ward Hartenstein (bottom of fourth house)
- Teddy World: Shawn Brenneman
- The Asylum Pro: Steve Sullivan
